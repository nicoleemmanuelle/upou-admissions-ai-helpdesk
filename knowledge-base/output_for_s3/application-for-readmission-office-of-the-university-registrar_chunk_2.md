## Topic: Application for Readmission - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page explains the application process for readmission through the Student Portal, including required documents, payment, approval, and enrollment after approval.

### Admission Requirements
- Log in to the Student Portal and select **Application for readmission**.
- Upload the required documents.
- Upload proof of payment of **₱225.00**.
- If the student has exceeded the **Maximum Residency Rule (MRR)**, a separate application for an **extension or waiver of the MRR** is required.

### Application Procedure
1. Log in to the **Student Portal**.
2. Click **Application for readmission**.
3. Upload the required documents and proof of payment.
4. Wait for the approval of the application.
5. Once approved, proceed with enrollment.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Applications received **after the deadline** will be processed for the **next term**.
- Exception: applications from students with **graduating status** and those scheduled to take the **comprehensive examination** may be handled differently.

### Program-Specific Notes
- **Readmission from Dismissed Status:** Applies to students dismissed from their college or the University for reasons such as failing to meet retention requirements. Approval is delegated to the **Vice Chancellors for Academic Affairs (VCAA)**.
- **Readmission from Permanent Disqualification (PDQ):** Applies to students permanently disqualified from the University. Reinstatement/readmission approval is delegated to the **Vice Chancellors for Academic Affairs**.

### FAQs
Q: What happens if I apply after the deadline?
A: Applications received after the deadline will be processed for the next term, except for students with graduating status and those scheduled to take the comprehensive examination.

Q: What if I have exceeded the Maximum Residency Rule (MRR)?
A: You must submit a separate application for an extension or waiver of the MRR.
