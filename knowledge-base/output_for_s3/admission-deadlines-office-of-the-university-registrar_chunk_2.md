## Topic: Admission Deadlines - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
The page provides admission deadline information for Academic Year 2026–2027 and notes on which applicants should apply through the UP College Admissions office.

### Admission Requirements
- New Senior HS graduates or incoming freshmen must apply through the UP College Admissions, Office of Admissions, Kalaw corner Quirino Street, U.P. Diliman, Quezon City.
- Applicants should check the UP College Admissions website for the deadline for filing applications: https://upcollegeadmissions.up.edu.ph/

### Application Procedure
1. Determine whether you are a new Senior High School graduate or incoming freshman.
2. If yes, apply through the UP College Admissions, Office of Admissions.
3. Visit the UP College Admissions website to check the application deadline.
4. Review whether your intended program accepts admission for the relevant trimester/semester intake.

### Important Dates and Timelines

### FAQs
Q: Where should new Senior HS graduates or incoming freshmen apply?
A: They should apply through the UP College Admissions, Office of Admissions, Kalaw corner Quirino Street, U.P. Diliman, Quezon City.

Q: Where can applicants check the filing deadline?
A: Applicants should visit https://upcollegeadmissions.up.edu.ph/ for the deadline for filing of applications.

Q: Which programs do not have 2nd Trimester/Semester intake/admission?
A: BAMS, DCS, GCAS, MAS, MIS, MDC, DIH, MIH, MAN, DCOMM, and D/MRDM.
