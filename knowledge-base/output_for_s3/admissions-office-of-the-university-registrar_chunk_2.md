## Topic: Admissions - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
UP Open University (UPOU) admits students to undergraduate, graduate certificate, graduate diploma, master’s, doctorate, associate, and certification programs.

### Admission Requirements
- Applicants may be:
  - Philippine-based or offshore-based applicants
  - Foreign nationals
  - Undergraduate transfer students from another UP unit or university to UPOU
- Applicants must satisfy the minimum admission requirements of the specific program applied for.
- Applicants must not be a current student of another university or college, including those enrolled, on residency, on leave of absence (LOA), or AWOL.
- Applicants must submit hard copies of all admission documents.
- Applicants must be cleared from all universities previously attended before applying and being admitted to UPOU.
- Concurrent enrollment in two or more degree programs is not allowed.
- Once admitted and enrolled at UPOU, students may not enroll in any other degree program at another university or in any other UP constituent university.

### Application Procedure
1. Ensure you meet the minimum admission requirements of the program you want to apply for.
2. Confirm that you are not currently enrolled in any other university or college.
3. Prepare all required admission documents in hard copy.
4. Submit the admission documents to UPOU as required by the program/application process.
5. If you are a foreign applicant, note that UPOU does not assist in the issuance of a Student Visa (9F).

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific application deadlines or enrollment dates were provided in the source.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Admission requirements vary by program; applicants must meet the minimum requirements specified for the program applied for.
- UPOU does not require students to be in residence in the Philippines.
- Because of this, UPOU does not assist in the issuance of a Student Visa (9F) for foreign applicants.

### FAQs
Q: Who can apply for admission to UPOU?
A: Philippine-based and offshore-based applicants, foreign nationals, and undergraduate transfer students from another UP unit or university to UPOU.

Q: Can I apply if I am currently enrolled in another university?
A: No. Applicants must not be a current student of another university or college, including those on residency, LOA, or AWOL.

Q: Does UPOU assist foreign students in getting a Student Visa (9F)?
A: No. UPOU does not require students to be in residence in the Philippines and therefore cannot assist in the issuance of a Student Visa (9F).

Q: Can I enroll in another degree program while studying at UPOU?
A: No. UPOU does not allow concurrent enrollment in two or more degree programs, and enrolled students may not register in another degree program at another university or UP constituent university.
