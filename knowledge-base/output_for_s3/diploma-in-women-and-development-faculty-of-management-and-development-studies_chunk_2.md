## Topic: Diploma in Women and Development - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Diploma in Women and Development is a post-baccalaureate program. Admission is based on academic qualifications, references, and, for foreign applicants, English proficiency. The program also includes a fieldwork/practicum component with specific placement and supervision requirements.

### Admission Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree, preferably in Social Sciences, from a recognized academic institution
- Satisfactory academic record
- High level of intellectual capacity and integrity
- For foreign applicants: high English proficiency, evidenced by a TOEFL certification
- Undergraduate credentials will be reviewed
- References from former professors and professional supervisors will be reviewed

### Application Procedure
1. Submit the required admission documents.
2. The Program Admission Committee, headed by the Program Chair of the Diploma in Women and Development Program, reviews the submitted documents.
3. The committee examines undergraduate credentials and references.
4. The OUR informs the applicant of the admission result.
5. If all requirements are complete, the applicant is admitted as a regular student.
6. If some requirements are still missing, the applicant may be given provisional status and allowed one year to complete the missing requirements.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific application deadline is stated.
- Provisional students are given one year to complete the missing admission requirements.
- The program can be completed in three to four semesters.
- Fieldwork requires 170 hours, usually during regular working hours.

### Program-Specific Notes
- The program requires one field instruction course.
- Fieldwork is an integral part of the program and requires face-to-face supervision.
- Students whose work schedules do not allow fieldwork at another agency may apply for a work-study scheme.
- Under the work-study scheme, fieldwork may be conducted in the student’s own agency if it meets the fieldwork agency guidelines.
- Only students who have completed their core courses are allowed to take the fieldwork subject.
- The agency fieldwork supervisor should have at least a master’s degree in any development discipline.
- The fieldwork agency must meet minimum approval considerations, including:
  - Interest in providing field instruction
  - Preferably affiliated with a licensed and accredited institution/agency or other accredited organizations
  - Existing programs and services aligned with fieldwork objectives
  - At least one qualified and willing licensed social worker to provide instruction/supervision

### FAQs
Q: How are applicants evaluated for admission?
A: Through examination of undergraduate credentials and review of references from former professors and professional supervisors.

Q: What happens if an applicant has incomplete admission requirements?
A: The applicant may be admitted provisionally and given one year to complete the missing requirements.

Q: Is fieldwork required?
A: Yes. The program includes one field instruction course requiring 170 hours of agency-based fieldwork.

Q: Can fieldwork be done at the student’s workplace?
A: Yes, through a work-study scheme, if the workplace meets the guidelines to become a fieldwork agency.
