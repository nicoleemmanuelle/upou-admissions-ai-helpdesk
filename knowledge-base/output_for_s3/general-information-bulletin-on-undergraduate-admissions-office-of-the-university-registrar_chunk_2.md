## Topic: General Information Bulletin on Undergraduate Admissions - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
UPOU undergraduate admissions are handled through the Office of the University Registrar and the Online Application System. Applicants must meet program-specific eligibility requirements, complete the required readiness module, and submit complete documentary requirements within the deadline for the preferred term of admission. Only applicants with approved applications and complete admission requirements may enroll.

### Admission Requirements
- General eligibility for undergraduate admission may include:
  - A high school diploma or equivalent through:
    - the K to 12 curriculum,
    - the old basic education curriculum, or
    - passing the DepEd ALS A&E Test for the secondary level.
- Program-specific academic qualifications:
  - For BES or BAMS:
    - Must meet one of the following:
      - Passed UPCAT with UPG of 2.8 or better, or
      - Completed at least 33 college units with GWA of 2.50 or better from another UP unit, or GWA of 2.0 or better from a non-UP institution, or
      - Be a graduate of UPOU’s Associate in Arts (AA) program.
    - Must complete the UPOU Distance Education (DE) Readiness Module.
  - For AA:
    - Must meet one of the following:
      - Take and pass UPOU’s Undergraduate Assessment Test (UgAT), unless the applicant has a UPCAT UPG of 2.800 or better, or
      - Completed at least 33 college units with GWA of 2.5 or better from another UP unit, or GWA of 2.0 or better from a non-UP institution.
    - Must complete the UPOU Distance Education Readiness (DER) Module.
    - May be advised to complete bridge courses if necessary.
- Transfer admission:
  - Applicants must satisfy both university requirements and the requirements of the college/degree program they are transferring to.
  - Must have an average of “2”, “86%”, or “B”, or better for all collegiate units earned outside the UP System.
  - Must complete at least 50% of the units required for the course in the UP System.
- Additional notes:
  - UPOU does not allow concurrent enrollment in two or more degree programs.
  - Students who have exceeded the Maximum Residency Rule (MRR) may not register again in the same program.
  - Admission is not denied on the basis of age, sex, nationality, religious belief, or political affiliation.

### Application Procedure
1. Submit the application through the Online Application System and upload the required documents.
2. Complete the DE Readiness Module (DERM/DER Module); the link is sent by email from admissions.
3. Wait for the email from the Office of the University Registrar regarding the admission result.
4. If admitted, mail or bring the complete and valid original admission requirements to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines, on or before the end of the trimester/semester of first enrollment.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Application documents must be received in hard copy by the Office of the University Registrar within the deadline for admission for the preferred term.
- Applicants should follow the deadline for submission of applications stated in the academic calendar posted on the OUR website.
- Admission results are released after evaluation by the Undergraduate Admissions Committee and before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.
- Complete and valid admission requirements must be submitted on or before the end of the trimester/semester in which the student is first enrolled.

### Fees
- Application fee:
  - PHP 350.00 for applicants based in the Philippines
  - USD 75.00 for applicants based abroad
  - Non-refundable

### Program-Specific Notes
- BES and BAMS applicants:
  - Must have the required secondary education credential and meet one of the listed academic qualification routes.
  - Must complete the DE Readiness Module.
- AA applicants:
  - Must take the UgAT unless exempted by a qualifying UPCAT UPG.
  - UgAT can be taken only once.
  - May be advised to take bridge courses.
  - Must complete the DER Module.
- UPCAT qualifiers for BAMS or BES:
  - Must wait for an email from the Office of the University Registrar for confirmation instructions.
  - Must prepare:
    - Original Form 137
    - Original Form 138
    - Photocopy of NSO/PSA birth certificate
    - Two identical 2x2 photos and two identical 1x1 photos
  - Must complete the online Distance Education Readiness Module (DERM).
- Foreign applicants:
  - UPOU does not require students to reside in the Philippines.
  - UPOU cannot assist in the issuance of a 9(F) Student Visa.
  - Must submit authenticated scholastic records and passport/birth documentation as applicable.

### FAQs
Q: When is an application considered complete?
A: Only when the Office of the University Registrar receives the documentary requirements in hard copy format within the deadline for the preferred term of admission.

Q: Can an applicant enroll before admission requirements are complete?
A: No. Only applicants with approved applications and complete admission requirements may enroll.

Q: Is the UgAT required for all applicants?
A: No. It is required for AA applicants unless they qualify for exemption through a UPCAT UPG of 2.800 or better.

Q: Can the UgAT be taken more than once?
A: No. The UgAT can be taken only once.

Q: Does UPOU allow concurrent enrollment in another degree program?
A: No. Once admitted and enrolled at UPOU, a student may not enroll in another degree program in another university or another UP constituent university.
