## Topic: Master of Climate and Disaster Resilience - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Master of Climate and Disaster Resilience (MCDR) is a graduate program with an exit option to earn the Graduate Certificate in Climate and Disaster Resilience (GCCDR) after completing 15 units, if the student cannot finish the full master’s program.

### Admission Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree from a recognized institution of higher learning.
- Recommendation from two (2) previous professors or immediate supervisors.
- Statement of purpose, maximum 1000 words, outlining:
  - academic background
  - career goals
  - motivations for pursuing a graduate degree in resilience
- Concept proposal, maximum 500 words, outlining the applicant’s vision for their field project in Climate and Disaster Resilience.

### Application Procedure
1. Submit an application for admission to the program.
2. Applications are evaluated by the Program Admission Committee (PAC), composed of faculty from FMDS and representatives from UP Resilience Institute (UPRI).
3. If admitted, complete online registration through the Student Portal.
4. Follow the online registration instructions provided by the university.
5. Refer to the Enrollment FAQs for additional questions.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Courses are delivered on a semestral basis.
- Courses during the mid-year term are delivered through virtual classrooms in MyPortal.
- No specific application deadline was stated in the provided content.

### Program-Specific Notes
- The program has two tracks:
  - Strategic Practice Track
  - Community Resilience and Empowerment Track
- Both tracks are taken after the second semester of coursework.
- To earn the master’s degree, a student must complete 34 units of specified coursework with a general weighted average of 2.0 or better.
- Students who exit after completing 15 units may be awarded the GCCDR.
- Retention, cross-registration, transfer/shifting, and crediting of microcredentials follow existing UP Open University and UP System rules.
- Courses from UP constituent universities and other universities may be considered for transfer of credit, subject to existing policies.

### FAQs
Q: Who evaluates applications for the MCDR program?
A: Applications are evaluated by a Program Admission Committee (PAC) made up of FMDS faculty members and representatives from UP Resilience Institute (UPRI).

Q: What are the minimum admission documents?
A: Applicants need a bachelor’s degree, two recommendations, a statement of purpose, and a concept proposal.

Q: Can a student earn a credential without completing the full master’s program?
A: Yes. Students may earn the Graduate Certificate in Climate and Disaster Resilience (GCCDR) after completing 15 units of coursework and exiting the program.

Q: What is required to earn the master’s degree?
A: Completion of 34 units of specified coursework and a general weighted average of 2.0 or better.
