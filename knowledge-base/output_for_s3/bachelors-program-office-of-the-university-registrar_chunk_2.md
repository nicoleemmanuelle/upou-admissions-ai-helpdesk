## Topic: Bachelors Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page provides the admission procedure for applicants to UPOU bachelor’s programs, specifically for:
- AA graduates
- Transferees to BAMS and BES
- Applicants with previous degree/s

### Admission Requirements
- Complete and valid admission documents must be submitted by mail after completing the online application.
- Applicants must complete the DE Readiness Module (DERM) as part of the admission process.

### Application Procedure
1. Apply for admission and upload the admission requirements through the Online Application System (OAS).
2. Monitor the progress of the application through the OAS.
3. If you are a UPOU alumnus or former UPOU student applying for a new degree program, access the Online Application System through the Student Portal and do not create a new OAS account.
4. After completing the online application, mail the complete and valid admission documents to:

   The Admission Section  
   Office of the University Registrar  
   UP Open University  
   Los Baños, Laguna 4031  
   Philippines

5. Complete the DE Readiness Module (DERM). The link will be emailed by admissions.
6. Wait for an email from the Office of the University Registrar (OUR) regarding the admission results.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Refer to the UPOU Academic Calendar for the deadline for submission of application documents.
- Admission results are released after evaluation by the Undergraduate Admissions Committee.
- Results are released before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.

### Program-Specific Notes
- This admission procedure applies only to:
  - AA graduates
  - Transferees to BAMS and BES
  - Applicants with previous degree/s
- UPOU alumni and former UPOU students applying for a new degree program must use the Student Portal instead of creating a new OAS account.

### FAQs
Q: Where should admission documents be sent?
A: Mail the complete and valid admission documents to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines.

Q: How will I receive the DERM link?
A: The link to the DE Readiness Module (DERM) will be emailed by admissions.

Q: When will admission results be released?
A: Results will be released after evaluation by the Undergraduate Admissions Committee and before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.

Q: Where can I find the deadline for submitting application documents?
A: Refer to the UPOU Academic Calendar for the deadline for submission of application documents.
