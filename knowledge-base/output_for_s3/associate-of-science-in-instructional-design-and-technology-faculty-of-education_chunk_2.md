## Topic: Associate of Science in Instructional Design and Technology - Faculty of Education

### Overview
- The Associate of Science in Instructional Design and Technology (ASIDT) is a pre-baccalaureate program.
- It is designed to prepare students for further study and/or immediate employment in instructional design and related education work.
- The program is online and follows guided independent study and collaborative inquiry.
- The ASIDT program can be ladderized toward UPOU baccalaureate programs such as:
  - Bachelor of Education Studies (BES)
  - Bachelor of Multimedia Studies (BAMS)

### Admission Requirements
Applicants who may apply:
1. High school or senior high school graduates who have never enrolled in college
2. Passers of the DepEd Alternative Learning System Accreditation and Equivalency (ALS A&E) Test for secondary level
3. Passers of UPCAT with a minimum score of 2.800
4. Transferees with college units
5. Graduates of other undergraduate degrees

To be admitted to the program, the applicant must:
- Submit complete documentary requirements for admission before the application deadline
- Take and pass the UgAT, if required
- Complete the UPOU Distance Education (DE) Readiness Module
- Submit an admission essay

Additional note:
- UPCAT passers, applicants who meet transfer requirements, and degree holders may no longer be required to take the UgAT.

### Application Procedure
1. Prepare and submit the complete documentary requirements before the application deadline.
2. Take and pass the UgAT, if required.
3. Complete the UPOU Distance Education (DE) Readiness Module.
4. Submit the admission essay.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Documentary requirements must be submitted before the application deadline.
- UgAT exam details will be emailed by the Admission Section of the Office of the University Registrar.
- The DE Readiness Module link will be emailed by Admissions.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Admission is through the UgAT.
- Some applicants may be exempted from taking the UgAT:
  - UPCAT passers
  - Applicants who meet transfer requirements
  - Degree holders
- All courses are taught online.

### FAQs
Q: Who can apply to the ASIDT program?
A: High school or senior high school graduates with no college enrollment, ALS A&E secondary passers, UPCAT passers with a minimum score of 2.800, transferees with college units, and graduates of other undergraduate degrees.

Q: Is the UgAT always required?
A: No. UPCAT passers, applicants who meet transfer requirements, and degree holders may no longer be required to take the UgAT.

Q: What other admission steps are required?
A: Applicants must complete the DE Readiness Module and submit an admission essay, in addition to submitting complete documentary requirements.
