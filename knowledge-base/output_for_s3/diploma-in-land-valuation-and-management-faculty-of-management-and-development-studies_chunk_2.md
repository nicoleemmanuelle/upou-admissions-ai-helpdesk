## Topic: Diploma in Land Valuation and Management - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Diploma in Land Valuation and Management (DLVM) is a ladderized program that can lead to the Master in Land Valuation and Management (MLVM). It is designed for applicants with or without a background in land valuation and management.

### Admission Requirements
- Applicant must have a baccalaureate degree in any field.

### Application Procedure
- For admission-related details, applicants are directed to visit the UPOU website.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- The program is delivered on a semestral basis (16 weeks).
- The diploma program consists of 24 units and can be completed in two years of part-time study.

### Program-Specific Notes
- DLVM is ladderized with the MLVM program; graduates of DLVM may continue to MLVM.
- The program is composed of eight courses, each worth three units.
- Courses are delivered using various technologies through MyPortal, the UPOU learning management system.
- Students who start in the first semester or second semester are expected to follow different course sequences, but the sequences are not provided in the source text.
