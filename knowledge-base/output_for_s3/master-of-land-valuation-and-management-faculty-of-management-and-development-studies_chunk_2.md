## Topic: Master of Land Valuation and Management - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Master of Land Valuation and Management (MLVM) is a ladderized program. Applicants must first complete the Diploma in Land Valuation and Management (DLVM) before applying to MLVM. The program is intended for professional land valuers and property managers, and also accepts applicants with no background in land valuation and management who wish to enter the profession.

### Admission Requirements
- Must have a DLVM degree.
- Must have an average of 2.0 or better in DLVM courses.
- If the DLVM average is lower than 2.0, the applicant must take and pass a qualifying exam covering all DLVM courses.

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the DLVM program and earn the DLVM degree.
2. Check whether your DLVM average is 2.0 or better.
3. If your average is below 2.0, take and pass the qualifying exam covering all DLVM courses.
4. Apply for admission to the MLVM program.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- DLVM can be completed in two years of part-time study.
- MLVM can be completed with an additional year of part-time study after DLVM.
- Courses are delivered on a semestral basis.

### Program-Specific Notes
- MLVM is a ladderized program with DLVM as the prerequisite.
- MLVM students complete an additional year that includes thesis work and additional courses.
- Courses are delivered using various technologies, with virtual classrooms hosted in MyPortal.
- For admission-related details, the page directs applicants to the UPOU website.

### FAQs
Q: Who can apply for the MLVM program?
A: Applicants who have completed the DLVM degree and meet the required academic average, or those who pass the qualifying exam if their DLVM average is below 2.0.

Q: What if my DLVM average is below 2.0?
A: You must take and pass a qualifying exam covering all DLVM courses.

Q: How long does it take to complete the program?
A: DLVM takes two years of part-time study, and MLVM takes an additional year of part-time study.
