## Topic: Associate of Arts in Digital Entrepreneurship - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Associate of Arts in Digital Entrepreneurship (AADE) is an associate program that prepares students for baccalaureate studies and may also equip them for employment or careers in digital entrepreneurship and related fields.

### Admission Requirements
- High School or Senior High School graduates who have never enrolled in college; admission is through Undergraduate Admission Test (UgAT)
- Passers of the Department of Education’s Alternative Learning System Accreditation and Equivalency (ALS A&E) Test for secondary level; admission is through UgAT
- Passers of UP College Admission Test (UPCAT) with a minimum score of 2.80
- Transferees with college units
- Graduates of other undergraduate degrees

### Application Procedure
1. Submit the complete documentary requirements for admission before the application deadline.
2. Take and pass the Undergraduate Admission Test (UgAT). Exam details will be emailed by admissions.
3. Complete the UPOU Distance Education Readiness Module (DERM). The DERM link will be emailed by admissions.
4. Submit an admission essay of 500 words indicating:
   - your purpose for applying to the distance education programs
   - your plans after completion of the associate program at the UP Open University

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Complete documentary requirements must be submitted before the application deadline.
- Associate programs are designed to be completed in 2 years for full-time students.
- Maximum residency rule (MRR): 7 years

### Program-Specific Notes
- AADE follows Republic Act No. 10647 (Ladderized Education Act of 2014).
- AADE is in sync with Republic Act No. 10968, which institutionalizes the Philippine Qualifications Framework (PQF).
- The program consists of 72 units of coursework:
  - GE Courses: 30 units
  - Government-mandated courses: 3 units, along with PE and NSTP
  - Common courses: 12 units
  - Concentration courses: 27 units
- Online registration is done through the Student Portal.
- Applicants can refer to the UPOU website for admission-related details.
