## Topic: Diploma in Land Use Planning - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
- The Diploma in Land Use Planning (DLUP) is intended for local government planners and related technical personnel.
- Admission applications are evaluated by an admission committee.

### Admission Requirements
- An undergraduate degree in any field of study is required.
- Priority is given to:
  - Municipal Planning and Development Coordinators
  - City Planning and Development Coordinators
  - Provincial Planning and Development Coordinators
  - Other technical personnel of government planning offices

### Application Procedure
1. Submit an application for admission.
2. Applications will be evaluated by the admission committee.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- The program can be completed in 3 to 4 semesters, or about 1.5 to 2 years.

### Program-Specific Notes
- DLUP is primarily delivered online.
- The Urban Planning Workshop course requires face-to-face meetings.
- Graduates may be eligible to take the Environmental Planners Licensure Examination (EPLE), subject to the requirements of Republic Act No. 10587.
- The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) and the Professional Regulatory Board (PRB) of Environmental Planning are the legal authorities on matters related to the EPLE.
- The program consists of 21 units of core and major courses.

### FAQs
Q: Is there a specific undergraduate degree required for admission?
A: No. An undergraduate degree in any field of study is accepted.

Q: Who gets priority for admission?
A: Priority is given to municipal, city, and provincial planning and development coordinators, as well as other technical personnel of government planning offices.

Q: How long does the program take to complete?
A: It can be completed in 3 to 4 semesters, or about 1.5 to 2 years.
