## Topic: Diploma in International Health - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
- The Diploma in International Health (DIH) is intended for entering and mid-career health professionals.
- It is relevant for professionals seeking graduate-level training for supervisory work in government health departments, local and international NGOs, and academic careers in international health.

### Admission Requirements
Applicants must submit:
- Official transcript of records
- Certified true copy of the college diploma
- Updated curriculum vitae
- Certificate of training or employment describing the nature of the applicant’s work

Applicants must also be:
- Graduates of approved medical schools, or graduates with professional training in nursing, dentistry, veterinary medicine, allied medical professions, social work, and related fields, with at least a baccalaureate degree in science
- Have at least one (1) year of experience or previous training in public health, evidenced by a certificate of training or employment describing the nature of the applicant’s work
- Meet other criteria set by the Admissions Committee

### Application Procedure
1. Prepare the required documents:
   - Official transcript of records
   - Certified true copy of the college diploma
   - Updated curriculum vitae
   - Certificate of training or employment
2. Submit the application and supporting documents.
3. Wait for evaluation by the Admissions Committee and comply with any additional criteria they may require.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- A DIH graduate may seek admission into the Master’s program within three years of earning the DIH.
- Beyond three years after earning the DIH, the graduate must pass the qualifying examination for admission to the Master’s program.
- Courses are delivered on a semestral basis.

### Program-Specific Notes
- A student who completes the 24-unit DIH program may opt to graduate or proceed to the Master of International Health (MIH) program.
- To proceed to MIH, the student must comply with MIH admission requirements and pass the Qualifying Exam.
- The MIH pathway requires an additional 12 units: one core course, one major course, and six units of thesis.

### FAQs
Q: Can a DIH graduate proceed to the Master of International Health (MIH)?
A: Yes. A DIH graduate may proceed to MIH upon complying with the MIH admission requirements and passing the Qualifying Exam.

Q: How long does a DIH graduate have to seek admission to the Master’s program without taking a qualifying exam?
A: Within three years of earning the DIH. After three years, the qualifying examination is required.

Q: What documents are required for DIH application?
A: Applicants must submit an official transcript of records, certified true copy of the college diploma, updated curriculum vitae, and a certificate of training or employment.
