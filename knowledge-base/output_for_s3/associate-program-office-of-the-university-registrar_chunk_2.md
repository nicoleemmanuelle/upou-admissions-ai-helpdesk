## Topic: Associate Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page provides the admission procedure for the Associate Program at UP Open University (UPOU), including required submission steps and evaluation-related requirements.

### Admission Requirements
- Complete and valid admission documents must be uploaded through the Online Application System.
- Complete and valid admission documents must also be mailed to the Office of the University Registrar after online application is accomplished.
- If required, applicants must take the Undergraduate Assessment Test (UgAT).
- Applicants must complete the DE Readiness Module (DERM).

### Application Procedure
1. Apply for admission and upload the admission requirements through the Online Application System (OAS).
2. Monitor the progress of the application through the OAS.
3. If the applicant is a UPOU alumnus or former UPOU student applying for a new degree program, access the Online Application System through the Student Portal and do not create a new OAS account.
4. After accomplishing the online application, mail the complete and valid admission documents to:

   The Admission Section  
   Office of the University Registrar  
   UP Open University  
   Los Baños, Laguna 4031  
   Philippines
5. If required, take the Undergraduate Assessment Test (UgAT). Details will be emailed by admissions.
6. Complete the DE Readiness Module (DERM). The link will be emailed by admissions.
7. Wait for an email from the Office of the University Registrar regarding the admission results.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Refer to the UPOU Academic Calendar for the deadline for submission of application documents.
- Admission results are released after evaluation by the Undergraduate Admissions Committee and before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.

### Program-Specific Notes
- UPOU alumni and former UPOU students applying for admission to a new degree program must use the Student Portal instead of creating a new OAS account.
- UgAT is required only if specified by admissions.
- DERM completion is required as part of the admission process.
- The page was updated on 26 April 2021.

### FAQs
Q: Where should admission documents be sent?
A: Admission documents should be mailed to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines.

Q: How will applicants know if they need to take the UgAT or complete DERM?
A: Details for the UgAT and the DERM link will be emailed by admissions.

Q: When are admission results released?
A: Results are released after evaluation by the Undergraduate Admissions Committee and before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.
