## Topic: Change Matriculation - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page provides the procedure for applying for a change of matriculation through the Student Portal.

### Admission Requirements
- No specific admission requirements are listed.
- Applicants must have access to the Student Portal / AIMS Student Portal.

### Application Procedure
1. Log in to the Student Portal and click on **Application for Change of Matriculation**.
2. Select the applicable type of application:
   - Cancellation of course(s)
   - Addition of course(s)
   - Combination of Cancellation and Addition of course(s)
3. Select the reason for each application from the dropdown list.
4. Click **Submit** after finalizing the application.
5. Wait for processing by the following offices in order:
   - Program Chair
   - Faculty Secretary
   - Dean
6. If approved, click **Proceed to Payment** and settle the fees reflected in the system.
   - If disapproved, email your Program Chair for clarification.
7. Upload your Proof of Payment (POP) to the system.
8. The Office of the University Registrar (OUR - Registration Section) verifies the validity of the payment.
9. The OUR facilitates the request once payment is verified as successful.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific deadlines or enrollment dates are stated.
- Students can view the status of their application through their AIMS Student Portal account.

### Fees
- Fees are payable only if the request is approved.
- The exact fee amount is not specified; it is reflected in the system.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Applications are processed in this order:
  1. Program Chair
  2. Faculty Secretary
  3. Dean
- For concerns regarding application status, contact your respective Faculty of Study (FOS).
