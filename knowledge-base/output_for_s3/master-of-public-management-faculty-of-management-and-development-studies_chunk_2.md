## Topic: Master of Public Management - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
- The Master of Public Management (MPM) is offered in distance education mode.
- It has three specializations:
  - Local Government and Regional Administration (LGRA)
  - Public Policy and Program Administration (PPPA)
  - Voluntary Sector Management (VSM)

### Admission Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree or equivalent from a recognized institution
- At least 2 years of work experience
- Must take and pass the MPM Graduate Admission Test (MPM-GAT), usually administered twice a year
- Required documents:
  - Duly accomplished application form for admission
    - Former UP students must indicate their student number
  - Official Transcript of Records:
    - 1 original and 1 photocopy
    - Must bear the school dry seal, Registrar’s signature in ink, and acceptable remarks
  - Two letters of recommendation from any of the following:
    - Employer/supervisor
    - Former professor
    - Former program adviser
    - UPOU Form 1a is used for this purpose
  - Two identical 2”x2” photos and two identical 1”x1” photos
    - Applicant’s name and the program applied for must be printed at the back
  - 500-word essay
    - Refer to item #30 of the application for admission form

### Application Procedure
1. Accomplish the application form for admission.
2. If you are a former UP student, indicate your student number on the form.
3. Prepare the required documents:
   - Official Transcript of Records
   - Two letters of recommendation
   - Photos
   - 500-word essay
4. Submit the application and supporting documents.
5. Take and pass the MPM Graduate Admission Test (MPM-GAT).

### Important Dates and Timelines
- MPM-GAT is usually administered twice a year
- The program must be completed within 7 years
- Students must complete 36 units with a GWA of 2.0 or better within the allowed period to qualify for the comprehensive examination

### Fees
- No fees mentioned in the provided content

### Program-Specific Notes
- The program is delivered through Distance Education (DE) using online platforms, mainly MOODLE/MyPortal LMS
- The MPM Program consists of:
  - 36 academic units
  - A written comprehensive examination
- Course structure includes:
  - 5 mandatory core courses
  - 2 survey courses
  - 3 specialization courses
  - 2 electives
- The comprehensive exam has two parts:
  - General field
  - Area of specialization
- A student is given two chances to pass both parts
- Failure to satisfy the comprehensive exam requirement results in permanent disqualification from the program
- Graduation requires passing the comprehensive examination

### FAQs
Q: How many specializations does the MPM program offer?
A: Three: Local Government and Regional Administration (LGRA), Public Policy and Program Administration (PPPA), and Voluntary Sector Management (VSM).

Q: How often is the MPM Graduate Admission Test administered?
A: It is usually administered twice a year.

Q: What is the minimum work experience required for admission?
A: At least two years of work experience.

Q: What is the completion period for the program?
A: Students must complete the program within seven years.

Q: What is required to qualify for the comprehensive examination?
A: Students must complete 36 units with a General Weighted Average (GWA) of 2.0 or better.
