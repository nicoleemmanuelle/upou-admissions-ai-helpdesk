## Topic: Master of Research and Development Management - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
- The MR&DM is a fully online graduate program.
- It is intended for researchers, scientists, engineers, and support personnel from higher education institutions (HEIs), government agencies, and private research organizations.
- The program is offered in two tracks:
  - Thesis track
  - Non-thesis track

### Admission Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree from a reputable higher education institution.
- Relevant work experience in a supervisory or managerial capacity.

### Application Procedure
- No detailed step-by-step application procedure was provided in the source text.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- The program can be completed in 7–8 semesters.
- The thesis and special problem components can be completed in two semesters.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Applicants without relevant work experience in a supervisory or managerial capacity will be admitted initially to the DR&DM program.
- DR&DM program graduates may qualify for admission to the MR&DM program if they have a GWA of 2.0 or better.
- Admitted DR&DM graduates must validate or re-enroll all courses taken more than five years before their admission to the MR&DM program.
- Other UP System rules on admission may apply.
- All subjects are offered online.
- The curriculum is 36 units and includes:
  - Thesis track: requires application of appropriate research methodology to an inquiry on processes, relations, structures, emerging innovations, and/or problems or gaps in the R&D organization.
  - Non-thesis track: requires a special problem applying learned knowledge and skills to address a specific problem, gap, or need in an organization or agency setting.
