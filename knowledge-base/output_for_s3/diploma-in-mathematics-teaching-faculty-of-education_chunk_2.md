## Topic: Diploma in Mathematics Teaching - Faculty of Education

### Overview
The Diploma in Mathematics Teaching (DMT) program is designed to upgrade the mathematics and pedagogical competencies of mathematics teachers in secondary schools, as well as those involved in mathematics curriculum and materials development.
