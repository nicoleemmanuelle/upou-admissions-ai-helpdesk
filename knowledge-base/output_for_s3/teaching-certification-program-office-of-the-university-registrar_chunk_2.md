## Topic: Teaching Certification Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
The page provides the admission procedure for the Teaching Certification Program at UP Open University (UPOU).

### Admission Requirements
- Complete and valid admission documents must be submitted.
- Admission requirements must be uploaded through the Online Application System (OAS).
- If the applicant is a UPOU alumnus or former UPOU student applying for a new degree program, the application must be accessed through the Student Portal instead of creating a new OAS account.

### Application Procedure
1. Apply for admission and upload the admission requirements through the Online Application System (OAS).
2. Monitor the progress of the application through the OAS.
3. After completing the online application, mail the complete and valid admission documents to:

   The Admission Section  
   Office of the University Registrar  
   UP Open University  
   Los Baños, Laguna 4031  
   Philippines

4. Complete the DE Readiness Module (DERM). The DERM link will be emailed by admissions.
5. Wait for an email from the Office of the University Registrar (OUR) regarding the admission results.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Refer to the UPOU Academic Calendar for the deadline for submission of application documents.
- Admission results will be released after evaluation by the PTC Admissions Committee and before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.

### Program-Specific Notes
- The DE Readiness Module (DERM) is required as part of the admission process.
- UPOU alumni and former UPOU students applying for a new degree program must use the Student Portal and should not create a new OAS account.

### FAQs

A: Mail the complete and valid admission documents to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines.

Q: When will admission results be released?
A: Results will be released after evaluation by the PTC Admissions Committee and before the registration/enrollment period of the applied term.

Q: How do UPOU alumni or former UPOU students apply for a new degree program?
A: They should access the online application system through the Student Portal and should not create a new OAS account.
