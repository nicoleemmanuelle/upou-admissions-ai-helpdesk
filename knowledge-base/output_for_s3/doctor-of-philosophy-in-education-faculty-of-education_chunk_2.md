## Topic: Doctor of Philosophy in Education - Faculty of Education

### Overview
- The program is designed primarily for teacher educators, policy makers, researchers, and other educational personnel involved in science and mathematics education.
- It aims to upgrade the capabilities of Regional Science Teaching Centers (RSTCs) and other Teacher Education Institutions (TEIs).
