## Topic: Master of Information Systems - Faculty of Information and Communication Studies

### Overview
The Master of Information Systems (MIS) program has specific admission qualifications and a required set of application documents. Applicants may submit applications online or by mail/personal delivery. There is no separate application procedure for international students, and no English language proficiency score is required for them.

### Admission Requirements
- Applicants with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Diploma in Computer Science, or other IT-related courses with a GWA of 2.00 or better are qualified for admission.
- Other applicants must have all of the following:
  - A bachelor’s degree in any field from any recognized institution with a GWA of 2.00 or better
  - Proficiency in at least one programming language
  - Two years of work experience related to Information Systems development
- Applicants who do not meet the above requirements will be advised to apply to the Diploma in Computer Science program.

### Application Procedure
1. Review the documentary requirements and application procedures.
2. Check the Academic Calendar to avoid missing the application deadline.
3. Prepare the required application documents.
4. Print a copy of the accomplished online application form or apply online.
5. Submit the application through mail or personally to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines.
6. If applying for transfer of credits, accomplish the transfer of credit form and submit it with a photocopy of the transcript of records to the Learning Center or provide the Faculty Secretary a soft copy of the documents.
7. For enrollment, access the Student Portal, request a password using your student number, log in, and use the Online Registration System (ORS) to select courses.
8. Pay the required fees through the preferred payment facility and upload a scanned copy/digital image of the payment slip.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Application deadline is found in the Academic Calendar.
- Late submissions are not allowed; the deadline is strictly followed.
- Formal application for transfer of credits must be submitted before the first semester of admission.
- Courses taken more than five years before the time of application require a validation exam.
- Students must pass the Comprehensive Exam before registering for IS 295b.
- The MIS Program has a maximum residency rule of 7 years.

### Fees
- An application fee is required and is non-refundable.
- During enrollment, fees are assessed in the Student Portal after course selection and must be paid through the preferred payment facility.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Required application documents:
  - Accomplished online application form
  - Original and photocopy of valid Transcript of Records (TOR) from all schools attended, with school dry seal, Registrar’s signature in ink, and acceptable remarks
  - Two letters of recommendation from an employer/supervisor, former professor, or former program adviser
  - Proof of payment of application fee
  - Two identical 2” x 2” photos and two 1” x 1” photos with name and program applied for printed at the back
  - Certificate of training/experience proving proficiency in at least one programming language
  - 500-word essay explaining why the applicant is applying and background in systems development
  - Photocopy of NSO Birth Certificate
  - Photocopy of government-issued IDs with photo
- International students:
  - No separate application procedure
  - No English language proficiency score required
- Transferees from other master’s programs:
  - Must accomplish the transfer of credit form
  - Must submit updated, valid, original TOR for evaluation
  - Up to 9 units may be eligible for transfer of credits
  - For UP transferees, grades must not be lower than 2.0 and courses must have been taken within the last five years
  - For non-UP transferees, grades must not be lower than 2.0 and a validation exam is required for every course
- Transfer of credits must be applied for online at least two weeks before the scheduled validation exam for FICS students.
- Electives:
  - MIS requires 9 units of elective courses
  - Electives may be taken from other UPOU graduate programs
  - Approval from the Program Chair is required for electives outside the recommended list
  - MIS students are not allowed to take PhD courses as electives

### FAQs
Q: Can I send my application through mail/personally?
A: Yes. Applications may be sent by mail or submitted personally to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines.

Q: Is there a different application procedure for international students?
A: No. International students follow the same requirements and procedure as other applicants.

Q: Are international students required to submit English language proficiency scores?
A: No. English language proficiency scores are not required for the MIS Program.

Q: Are transferees from other master’s programs (UP and Non-UP) allowed?
A: Yes. They must submit a transfer of credit form and a photocopy of their TOR for evaluation. Additional rules apply depending on whether the transferee is from UP or non-UP.

Q: When is the application deadline?
A: The deadline is listed in the Academic Calendar.

Q: Are late submissions of applications allowed?
A: No. Late submissions are not accepted.

Q: How can I check if my application was accepted?
A: Application status can be checked through the MIS Program application status page.

Q: What are the enrollment procedures?
A: Enrollment is done online through the Student Portal and Online Registration System (ORS), followed by payment through a payment facility and uploading proof of payment.
