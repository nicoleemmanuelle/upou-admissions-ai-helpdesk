## Topic: Master of Environment and Natural Resources Management - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The MENRM is a ladderized post-baccalaureate program. Applicants are admitted into the MENRM program and may later exit with a Diploma in Environment and Natural Resources Management (DENRM) after completing 24 units, or continue to the Master’s degree after completing 36 units with a GWA of 2.0 or better.

### Admission Requirements
- A baccalaureate degree in any field.
- Duly accomplished application form.
- Original copy of the official transcript of academic records stating:
  - degree obtained
  - date of graduation
- Two letters of reference from former professors or supervisors, using the UPOU standard format.
- Official receipt of the application fee paid at any Philippine National Bank (PNB) branch.

### Application Procedure
1. Accomplish the application form.
2. Submit the application form to the Office of the University Registrar.
3. Attach the required documents:
   - official transcript of records
   - two letters of reference
   - official receipt of application fee
4. Applications are screened by the Program Admission Committee constituted by the FMDS Dean.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Application for comprehensive examination must be submitted at least two months prior to the date of examination.
- Students must choose either the Upland Resources Management (URM) Track or Coastal Resources Management (CRM) Track at the end of the first semester of the first year, before online registration for the second semester.
- Re-admission crediting for DENRM graduates:
  - up to 24 units if re-admitted within three years from graduation
  - up to 9 units if re-admitted after three years but within five years from graduation

### Program-Specific Notes
- The program is ladderized and completely post-baccalaureate.
- Students are considered Master’s students while enrolled or in residence.
- Students may exit with the DENRM after completing 24 units:
  - 6 units core courses
  - 12 units major courses
  - 6 units elective courses
- The Master’s degree requires completion of 36 units:
  - 6 units core courses
  - 12 units major courses
  - 9 units elective courses
  - 6 units research courses
  - 3-unit Special Problem
- Students select one of two tracks after completing two core courses:
  - Upland Resources Management (URM)
  - Coastal Resources Management (CRM)
- For the Master’s degree, a GWA of 2.0 or better is required for graduation.
- Comprehensive examination requirements:
  - apply after completing satisfactorily 30 units of coursework
  - GWA of 2.0 or better required
  - must pass before enrolling in ENRM 290 Special Problem
  - only one re-examination may be allowed within a year, and not later than one year after the first examination
- Special Problem requirements:
  - must be a worthwhile contribution to environment or natural resources management
  - research methods may be quantitative or qualitative
  - presentation is scheduled at the end of the semester
  - a CD copy must be submitted to the Faculty Office

### FAQs
Q: What is the minimum educational qualification for admission?
A: A baccalaureate degree in any field.

Q: Can students earn a diploma instead of the master’s degree?
A: Yes. Students may exit the program and earn the DENRM after completing 24 units of specified courses.

Q: When should students apply for the comprehensive examination?
A: After completing satisfactorily 30 units of coursework, and the application must be submitted at least two months before the examination date.

Q: Can DENRM graduates re-enter the MENRM program?
A: Yes. Courses may be credited upon re-admission, depending on how long after graduation the student is re-admitted.
