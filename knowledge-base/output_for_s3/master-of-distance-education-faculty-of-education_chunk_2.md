## Topic: Master of Distance Education - Faculty of Education

### Overview
The provided content contains program-specific admissions and crediting rules for the Master of Distance Education (MDE), including conditions for admission/re-admission, course crediting from the Graduate Certificate in Distance Education (GCDE), and the comprehensive examination requirement.

### Admission Requirements
- For a GCDE graduate applying for admission or re-admission to the MDE program, the applicant must have a General Weighted Average (GWA) of 2.0 or better in the GCDE.
- Courses completed for the GCDE may be credited toward the MDE only if:
  - The course was taken within three years from the application for crediting; and
  - The student earned a grade of 2.0 or better in the course.
- If a course does not meet either of the above conditions, it may still be credited if the student takes and passes a validation exam for that course before or within the semester of admission to the MDE program.

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the GCDE, if applicable, and ensure the required GWA of 2.0 or better for admission or re-admission to the MDE program.
2. Apply for admission or re-admission to the MDE program.
3. If requesting credit for GCDE courses, submit the courses for evaluation against the crediting conditions:
   - Taken within the last three years; and
   - Completed with a grade of 2.0 or better.
4. If a course does not satisfy the crediting conditions, take and pass the validation exam for that course before or within the semester of admission.
5. Upon admission, up to 18 units of GCDE courses may be credited, subject to the stated conditions and relevant University policies.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- GCDE courses to be credited must have been taken within three years from the application for crediting.
- Validation exams for non-qualifying courses must be taken and passed before or within the semester of admission to the MDE program.
- The comprehensive examination is taken after completing all core courses (18 units) and elective courses (12 units).

### Program-Specific Notes
- The comprehensive examination is required after completing all core courses (18 units) and elective courses (12 units), with no grade lower than 2.0 in more than three courses.
- Students admitted to the MDE program may opt to exit and be awarded the GCDE, provided they complete the GCDE course requirements.
- Up to 18 units of GCDE courses may be credited toward the MDE, subject to the stated conditions and other relevant University policies.

### FAQs
Q: Can a student exit the MDE program and receive a GCDE?
A: Yes. Students admitted to the MDE program may opt to exit and be awarded the GCDE if they complete the GCDE course requirements.

Q: What is required for a GCDE graduate to apply for admission or re-admission to the MDE program?
A: The GCDE graduate must have a GWA of 2.0 or better in the GCDE.

Q: How many GCDE units may be credited to the MDE?
A: Up to 18 units of GCDE courses may be credited, subject to the stated conditions and relevant University policies.

Q: When can a validation exam be used for crediting a course?
A: A validation exam may be used if the course was not taken within three years of the crediting application or if the student did not earn a grade of 2.0 or better; the exam must be passed before or within the semester of admission to the MDE program.
