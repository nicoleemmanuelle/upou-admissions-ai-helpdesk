## Topic: Diploma in Social Work - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Diploma in Social Work is open to applicants with a bachelor’s degree in social work and to those without one, with different tracks for each. Applicants are evaluated based on undergraduate credentials and references. Foreign applicants must demonstrate high English proficiency through TOEFL certification.

### Admission Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree from a recognized academic institution.
- High intellectual capacity and integrity.
- For foreign applicants: high English proficiency, evidenced by TOEFL certification.
- Admission is assessed through:
  - Examination of the applicant’s undergraduate credentials.
  - Review of references from former professors and professional supervisors.

### Application Procedure
1. Submit the required admission documents.
2. The Program Admission Committee, headed by the Program Chair of the Diploma in Social Work Program, deliberates on the submitted documents.
3. The Office of the University Registrar (OUR) informs applicants of the admission result.
4. If all requirements are complete, the applicant is admitted as a regular student.
5. If requirements are incomplete, the applicant may be given provisional status and is allowed one year to complete the missing requirements.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Provisional students are given one year to complete the missing admission requirements.
- The program can be completed in five to six semesters on a part-time basis, taking six units per semester.

### Fees
- No admission fees mentioned.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Applicants without a bachelor’s degree in social work follow a different track.
- Students without a bachelor’s degree in social work who complete the program successfully may qualify for the Master in Social Work program.
- The diploma consists of:
  - 27 units for holders of a bachelor’s degree in social work.
  - 33 units for those without a bachelor’s degree in social work.
- Upon completion of all courses, students must take and pass a comprehensive examination.
- Field instruction is required:
  - Two field instruction courses for those with a BSSW, each requiring 320 hours in an assigned agency.
  - Five field instruction courses for those without a bachelor’s degree in social work who intend to pursue the Master of Social Work and complete the 1,000-hour professional training requirement.
- Students whose work schedules do not allow field instruction at another agency may apply for a work-study scheme, subject to fieldwork agency guidelines.
- Only students who have completed their core and specialization/survey courses may take FI subjects, except SW 280.1, which requires completion of core courses only.
