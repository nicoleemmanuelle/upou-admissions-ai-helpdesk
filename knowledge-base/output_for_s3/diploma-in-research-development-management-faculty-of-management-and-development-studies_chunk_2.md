## Topic: Diploma in Research & Development Management - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
- The Diploma in Research and Development (R&D) Management is a post-baccalaureate program.
- It is designed for managerial leadership development in R&D and technology development/utilization contexts.

### Admission Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree from a recognized higher education institution.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Admission status may be any of the following:
  - Regular admission: applicant has satisfied all program requirements.
  - Probationary admission: applicant did not satisfy the academic requirements; may be reclassified as regular after meeting the required GWA for the required number of units/courses.
  - Provisional admission: applicant met minimum requirements but failed to submit all required documents.
- All subjects are offered online.
- The program consists of 24 units of coursework and may be completed in four semesters.
- Students admitted prior to AY 2020–2021 should follow the old DR&DM curriculum.
- A request to take the 3-unit R&DM 220 and an elective to replace the 6-unit R&DM 220 must be submitted through channels to the Dean’s office.
