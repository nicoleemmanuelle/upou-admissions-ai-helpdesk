## Topic: Masters Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page provides the documentary admission requirements and application-related instructions for UP Open University master’s programs, including general requirements and additional requirements for specific programs.

### Admission Requirements
- Signed hard copy of the application form generated from the Online Application System.
- Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with the school’s dry seal, imprint, and the Registrar’s original ink signature.
- If multiple schools or degrees were attended/earned, submit the OTR from the most recent school, and it must reflect all credentials from previous schools/degrees, including completed and not completed programs.
- Two (2) letters of recommendation from an employer/supervisor, former professor, or former program adviser.
- Recommenders must use UPOU Form 1a.
- Original copy of birth certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
- One (1) 2" x 2" ID photo.
- Scanned copy of the OTR uploaded via the Online Application System.
- 500-word essay stating the reason for applying to UPOU’s distance education programs and plans after completing graduate studies.
- Proof of payment of the non-refundable application fee.
- NSO or PSA-issued birth certificate uploaded in the Online Application System.
- Government-issued identification cards.
- For foreign applicants: copy of passport data page showing date and place of birth, and birth certificate or equivalent authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post.

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the application form in the Online Application System.
2. Print and sign the hard copy of the application form generated from the system.
3. Mail the signed hard copy and other required mailed documents to the Office of the University Registrar / Admission Section.
4. Upload the required digital documents in the Online Application System.
5. Submit proof of payment for the non-refundable application fee.
6. For recommendation letters, either:
   - mail them to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines; or
   - have recommenders email them from their institutional email address to [email protected].

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Photo must be taken within the last 4 months.
- Upon admission to the university, students must submit the original Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with valid remarks in hard copy.
- Applicants with international scholastic records must submit original records duly authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post in the applicant’s country of origin or legal residence.

### Fees
- Non-refundable application fee: PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens.
- Non-refundable application fee: USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens.

### Program-Specific Notes
- The OTR must bear the school’s dry seal, imprint, and the Registrar’s original ink signature.
- The OTR from the most recent school must reflect all credentials from previous schools/degrees, including incomplete programs.
- Recommendation letters must use UPOU Form 1a.
- Photo requirements:
  - Size: 2 x 2 inches
  - Colored
  - Neutral white or off-white background
  - Taken within the last 4 months
  - Full face centered, eyes visible, with no eyeglasses or accessories obscuring features
  - Handwritten name tag required in the format: First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, and Extension Name (if any), with signature over printed name
- For foreign applicants, passport and birth certificate documents must be authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post.
- Upon admission, hard copy submission of the original OTR with valid remarks is required.
- For applicants with international scholastic records, original records must be authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post in the applicant’s country of origin or legal residence.

### FAQs
Q: Are recommendation letters required?
A: Yes. Two letters of recommendation are required, and recommenders must use UPOU Form 1a.

Q: What is the application fee?
A: PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens and USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens. The fee is non-refundable.

Q: Can recommendation letters be submitted by email?
A: Yes. They may be emailed from the recommender’s institutional email address to [email protected].

Q: What documents must be uploaded online?
A: Scanned OTR, essay, proof of payment, NSO or PSA-issued birth certificate, government-issued IDs, and photo requirements.

Q: What documents must be mailed?
A: Signed hard copy of the application form, official transcript of records, two letters of recommendation, PSA birth certificate, and one 2" x 2" ID photo.

### Program-Specific Notes
- For MALLE, MASSE, MIH, MLVM, MAS (for GCAS Graduate), MRDM (for DRDM Graduate), and MENRM (for DENRM Graduate) programs, the following apply:
  - Submission of the other documentary admission requirements listed above is not required.
  - Required documents:
    - Signed hard copy of the application form generated from the Online Application System (mail to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar).
    - True copy of grades (TCG) for the Diploma program, requested from the OUR-UPOU Records Section.
    - 500-word essay indicating purpose for applying and plans after completion of graduate study, uploaded in the OAS.
    - Proof of payment of the non-refundable application fee: PHP 500.00 for applicants based in the Philippines, USD 100.00 for applicants based abroad. Payment procedures are available in the Online Application System.
