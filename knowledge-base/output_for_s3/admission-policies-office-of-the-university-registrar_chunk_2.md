## Topic: Admission Policies - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
The policy states that admission to the University System cannot be denied based on age, sex, nationality, religious belief, or political affiliation. Applicants must meet the minimum admission requirements of the program they are applying for, though relevant work experience may be considered in place of some minimum requirements by the Program Admission Committee.

### Admission Requirements
- Applicants must satisfy the minimum admission requirements specified for the program applied for.
- Relevant working experience may be considered in lieu of the minimum admission requirements, subject to Program Admission Committee approval.
- Transfer applicants must fulfill the requirements for admission as a transfer student in the receiving university.
- Applicants who do not meet the minimum program requirements may be admitted as non-degree students.

### Application Procedure
1. Apply to the intended program and ensure the minimum admission requirements are met.
2. If applying as a transfer student, satisfy the transfer admission requirements of the receiving university.
3. If minimum requirements are not fully met, the application may be considered for non-degree admission.
4. In exceptional cases, a visitor may be admitted to a class only upon authorization by the University Registrar, on the recommendation of the Dean or Director concerned, and only for up to five sessions.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific application deadlines or enrollment dates are mentioned.
- Visitor admission, when approved, is limited to not more than five sessions.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Minimum admission requirements vary by program.
- Relevant work experience may be accepted in lieu of minimum requirements, depending on the Program Admission Committee.
- Transfer admission is subject to the requirements of the receiving university.

### FAQs
Q: Can admission be denied based on personal characteristics?
A: No. Admission cannot be denied based on age, sex, nationality, religious belief, or political affiliation.

Q: Can work experience replace formal admission requirements?
A: Yes, relevant work experience may be considered in lieu of minimum admission requirements, subject to Program Admission Committee approval.

Q: Can a student transfer between UP constituent universities?
A: Yes, transfer is allowed if the student fulfills the admission requirements of the receiving university.

Q: What if an applicant does not meet the minimum program requirements?
A: The applicant may be admitted as a non-degree student.

Q: Can someone attend a class without matriculating?
A: Generally no. In exceptional cases, the University Registrar may authorize visitor admission for up to five sessions upon recommendation of the Dean or Director concerned.
