## Topic: Diploma Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Admission Requirements
- Signed hard copy of the application form generated from the Online Application System.
- Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with the school’s dry seal and imprint, and the Registrar’s original ink signature.
- If multiple schools or degrees were attended/earned, submit the OTR from the most recent school; it must reflect all credentials from previous schools/degrees, including completed and not completed degree programs.
- Two (2) Letters of Recommendation from an employer/supervisor, former professor, or former program adviser.
  - Recommenders must use UPOU Form 1a.
  - Recommendation may be mailed to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines, or emailed from the recommender’s institutional email address to [email protected].
- Original copy of birth certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
- One (1) piece 2″ x 2″ ID photo.
- For foreign applicants: copy of the passport’s data page (showing date and place of birth) and birth certificate or equivalent, both authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post.

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the Online Application System.
2. Prepare and mail the required hard-copy documents to the Office of the University Registrar.
3. Upload the required digital documents through the Online Application System.
4. Pay the non-refundable application fee and upload proof of payment.
5. Ensure recommendation letters are submitted either by mail or by email from the recommender’s institutional email address.
6. Upon admission, submit the required original documents in hard copy.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Photo must be taken within the last 4 months.
- Upon admission to the university, submit the original Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with valid remarks in hard copy.

### Fees
- Non-refundable application fee:
  - PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens
  - USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens

### Program-Specific Notes
- Essay requirement: a 500-word essay stating the reason for applying to UPOU’s distance education programs and plans after completing graduate studies.
- Photo requirements:
  - Size: 2×2 inches
  - Colored with a neutral white or off-white background
  - Full face centered, eyes visible, with no eyeglasses or accessories that obscure features
  - Handwritten name tag format: First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, and Extension Name (if any), with signature over printed name
- Uploaded documents must include:
  - Scanned copy of the OTR (not a photo of the TOR)
  - Essay
  - Proof of payment of the application fee
  - NSO or PSA-issued birth certificate
  - Government-issued identification cards
- For applicants with international scholastic records: submit the original records duly authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post located in the applicant’s country of origin or legal residence.

### FAQs
Q: What documents must be mailed to the Office of the University Registrar?
A: Signed application form, original OTR, two letters of recommendation, PSA birth certificate, and one 2″ x 2″ ID photo.

Q: What documents must be uploaded via the Online Application System?
A: Scanned OTR, 500-word essay, proof of payment of the non-refundable application fee, NSO or PSA-issued birth certificate, government-issued ID cards, and photo requirements documentation.

Q: What is the application fee?
A: PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens and USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens.

Q: What are the requirements for foreign applicants?
A: Passport data page showing date and place of birth, and birth certificate or equivalent, both authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post.
