## Topic: Doctorate Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page lists the documentary admission requirements and additional program-specific requirements for UPOU doctorate applicants. It also includes submission methods, application fee information, and special requirements for foreign applicants.

### Admission Requirements
- Signed hard copy of the application form generated from the Online Application System
- Official Transcript of Records (OTR)
  - Must bear the school’s dry seal, imprint, and the Registrar’s original ink signature
  - If multiple schools/degrees were attended, submit the OTR from the most recent school, and it must reflect all credentials from previous schools/degrees, completed and not completed
- Two (2) Letters of Recommendation
  - Recommenders may be an employer/supervisor, former professor, or former program adviser
  - Must use UPOU Form 1a
- Original copy of birth certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)
- One (1) piece 2" x 2" ID photo
- Scanned copy of the OTR uploaded in the Online Application System
- 500-word essay stating:
  - reason for applying to UPOU’s distance education programs
  - plans after completing graduate studies
- Proof of payment of the non-refundable application fee
  - PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens
  - USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens
- NSO or PSA-issued birth certificate
- Government-issued identification cards

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the application form in the Online Application System.
2. Print and sign the generated application form.
3. Prepare the required documents for mailing to the Office of the University Registrar.
4. Upload the required documents through the Online Application System.
5. Submit the proof of payment for the non-refundable application fee.
6. For recommendation letters, have recommenders either:
   - mail the signed UPOU Form 1a to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031, Philippines; or
   - email the recommendation from their institutional email address to [email protected]
7. Foreign applicants must also submit authenticated passport and birth certificate documents.
8. Upon admission, submit the original Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with valid remarks in hard copy.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific application deadline or enrollment schedule is stated on this page.
- Photo requirements specify the photo must be taken within the last 4 months.

### Fees
- Non-refundable application fee:
  - PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens
  - USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens

### Program-Specific Notes
- For all applicants:
  - Photo requirements:
    - Size: 2 x 2 inches
    - Colored, with a neutral white or off-white background
    - Taken within the last 4 months
    - Full face centered, eyes visible, with no eyeglasses or accessories obscuring features
    - Must include a handwritten name tag in the format: First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, and Extension Name (if any), with signature over printed name
- For foreign applicants:
  - Submit a copy of the passport data page showing date and place of birth
  - Submit birth certificate or equivalent
  - Both documents must be authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post
- For applicants with international scholastic records:
  - Submit original records duly authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post in the applicant’s country of origin or legal residence
- Additional requirements for the Doctorate Program must also be uploaded to the Online Application System if applicable
- If portfolio/sample work files exceed 10MB, upload a Word or PDF file containing publicly accessible links to the documents

#### Additional requirements for Doctor of Communication (DCOMM)
- Statement of Intent:
  - 2,000-word essay outlining the applicant’s research plan, proposed research framework, and problem/topics of interest
- Portfolio of Written Work:
  - Publications, research studies, media productions, scripts, and similar outputs
- English Language Proficiency:
  - Required for non-native English speakers who did not complete prior academic coursework in English
  - Acceptable exams include IELTS or TOEFL
- Computer and Internet Proficiency:
  - Demonstration of proficiency in computer technologies, the Internet, and other ICT tools essential for independent research
- Special Needs Statement:
  - Disclosure of any physical or mental conditions requiring special attention or support services

#### Additional requirements for PhD in Education
- Educational Background:
  - Master’s degree in basic mathematics/science or a sub-field of Education
- Alignment with Field of Specialization:
  - Master’s degree must include at least 15 units aligned with the PhD specialization
  - Otherwise, supplementary 200-level courses may be required
- Academic Performance:
  - General weighted average of at least 2.0 or B at the undergraduate level, or 1.75 or better at the master’s level
- Leadership Potential:
  - Demonstrated through statement of purpose, references, and other supporting documents
- Sample Works:
  - At least two samples of completed creative or research work at the master’s level
- Research Concept Paper:
  - Aligned with the intended dissertation topic
- DE Readiness Module:
  - Required for non-UPOU graduates
- Interview:
  - Applicants must demonstrate mental aptitude, language proficiency, and readiness for the program through an interview

#### Additional requirements for Doctor of Sustainability (DSus)
- Leadership Potential:
  - Demonstrated through the statement of purpose, references, and other supporting documents
- Scholarly Portfolio and/or Creative Work:
  - Includes scholarly or creative work such as publications, policy papers, technical reports, a Curriculum Vitae, and a Certificate of Employment showing relevant work/research experience over the past 5 years
- Dissertation Concept Proposal:
  - 2,000-word document outlining the research problem and proposed framework
- English Language Proficiency:
  - Required for non-native English speakers who did not take prior academic coursework in English
  - Accepted exams include IELTS or TOEFL
- Computer and Internet Proficiency:
  - Demonstration of capability to use ICT tools essential for independent research
- Special Needs Statement:
  - Disclosure of any conditions requiring special attention or support
- Midyear Intensive Program:
  - Attendance is required

### FAQs
Q: What is the application fee for the doctorate program?
A: PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens and USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens.

Q: What should be included in the essay?
A: A 500-word essay explaining the reason for applying to UPOU’s distance education programs and plans after completing graduate studies.

Q: What are the requirements for recommendation letters?
A: Two letters are required, using UPOU Form 1a, and may be submitted by mail or by email from the recommender’s institutional email address.

Q: Are there special requirements for foreign applicants?
A: Yes. They must submit a passport data page and birth certificate or equivalent, both authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post.

Q: Are there additional requirements for specific doctorate programs?
A: Yes. DCOMM, PhD in Education, and DSus each have additional documentary and academic requirements listed on the page.
