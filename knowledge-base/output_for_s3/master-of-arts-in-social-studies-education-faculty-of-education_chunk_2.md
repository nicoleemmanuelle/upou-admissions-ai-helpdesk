## Topic: Master of Arts in Social Studies Education - Faculty of Education

### Overview
- The program admits students first to the Diploma program.
- Students who wish to proceed to the MA program must complete all Diploma program courses with a GWA of 2.0 or better.

### Admission Requirements
- All students are admitted to the Diploma program first.
- To proceed to the MA program, students must:
  - Complete all courses for the Diploma program.
  - Earn a GWA of 2.0 or better.

### Application Procedure
1. Apply/admit into the Diploma program.
2. Complete all Diploma program courses.
3. Maintain a GWA of 2.0 or better to qualify for progression to the MA program.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific deadlines, enrollment periods, or application dates were provided in the source.

### Program-Specific Notes
- The Master’s program has two tracks:
  - Thesis option: 36 total units
    - 30 units of coursework
    - 6 units thesis
  - Non-thesis option: 37 total units
    - 33 units of coursework
    - 4 units special problem
    - Requires passing a comprehensive examination

### FAQs
Q: How do I qualify for the MA program?
A: Complete all courses in the Diploma program and obtain a GWA of 2.0 or better.

Q: Does the program have thesis and non-thesis options?
A: Yes. The thesis option requires 36 units total, while the non-thesis option requires 37 units total and includes a comprehensive examination.
