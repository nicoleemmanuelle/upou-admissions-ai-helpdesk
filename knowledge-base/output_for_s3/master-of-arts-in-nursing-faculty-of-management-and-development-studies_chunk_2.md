## Topic: Master of Arts in Nursing - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Master of Arts in Nursing (MAN) program is designed for professional nurses, including those working full-time, in remote areas, or abroad. It uses a flexible, semestral delivery mode through UPOU’s online learning system.

### Admission Requirements
- Applicant must be a professional nurse.
- At least **one year of clinical and nursing-related experience** is required for admission as a degree student.
- **GWA of 2.125 or better** is used as the initial screening cut-off.
- Applicants with borderline GWA are further evaluated using a **composite scoring system** based on:
  - GWA equivalent score
  - Length of experience
  - Nature of experience
- Applicants must have a **composite score of 2.0 or better** to be admitted as degree students.
- Applicants who do not meet the GWA cut-off or composite score may be admitted as **probationary students** and evaluated after **12 units**.
- If the required work experience is not met, the applicant may still be accepted as a **non-degree student** and re-evaluated after completing **50% of the program courses**.

### Application Procedure
1. Submit the application form indicating the intended major track:
   - Adult Health Nursing
   - Maternal and Child Nursing
   - Nursing Administration
   - Gerontology and Geriatrics Nursing
2. Applications are screened by the **Admission Committee**, headed by the Program Chair of the chosen major.
3. Applicants are evaluated based on:
   - GWA
   - Work experience
   - Composite score, if applicable
4. Qualified applicants are admitted either as:
   - Degree students
   - Probationary students
   - Non-degree students, depending on eligibility

### Important Dates and Timelines
- The program is delivered on a **semestral basis**.
- Probationary students are re-evaluated after **12 units**.
- Non-degree students are re-evaluated after completing **50% of the program courses**.
- The program requires completion of all coursework, followed by a **comprehensive examination** and **thesis defense**.

### Fees
- No admission fees or application fees were mentioned in the provided content.

### Program-Specific Notes
- Major tracks available:
  - Adult Health Nursing
  - Maternal and Child Nursing
  - Nursing Administration
  - Gerontology and Geriatrics Nursing
- Program structure:
  - **30 units** of coursework
  - **6 units** of thesis
  - Includes core, major, and elective courses
- Clinical practicum details:
  - Major courses include **32 hours** of practicum
  - Intensive clinical experience requires **60–80 hours**
  - Practicum is often conducted at **Philippine General Hospital**
  - Other affiliated sites include:
    - Philippine Heart Center
    - Dr. Jose Fabella Memorial Hospital
  - Offshore students may conduct practicum in their local setting if:
    - The setting is appropriate for the required competencies
    - A qualified preceptor with an **MA in Nursing** and relevant clinical experience is available and willing to supervise without compensation

### FAQs
Q: If I do not satisfy the required work experience, can I still be accepted to the program?  
A: Yes. You may still be accepted as a **non-degree student** and will be re-evaluated after taking **50% of the courses** in the program.

Q: What is the admission requirement for scholastic ability and potential for profitable study?  
A: The requirement is to maintain a **GWA of 2.0 or better** to stay in the program.

Q: How long is the clinical practicum?  
A: For major courses, it is **32 hours**, and **60–80 hours** for intensive clinical experience.

Q: Where is the clinical practicum conducted?  
A: It is usually conducted at the **Philippine General Hospital**, but may also be held at affiliated hospitals such as the **Philippine Heart Center** and **Dr. Jose Fabella Memorial Hospital**. Offshore students may also do practicum at their affiliated hospital.

Q: Are offshore students required to go home for the practicum?  
A: Not always. They may complete practicum in their local setting if the site is appropriate and a qualified preceptor is available.
