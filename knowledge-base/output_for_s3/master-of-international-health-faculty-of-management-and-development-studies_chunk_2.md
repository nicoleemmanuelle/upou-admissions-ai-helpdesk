## Topic: Master of International Health - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
The Master of International Health (MIH) is a graduate program in international health. For admissions purposes, the key pathway is through the Diploma in International Health (DIH) program.

### Admission Requirements
- Completion of the Diploma in International Health (DIH) program.
- Completion of the unit requirements for the Diploma program: 24 units.
- Compliance with the admission requirements for the MIH program.
- Passing the Qualifying Exam.

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the 24-unit Diploma in International Health (DIH) program.
2. Apply to proceed to the Master of International Health (MIH) program.
3. Comply with the MIH admission requirements.
4. Pass the Qualifying Exam.
5. Upon admission, take an additional 12 units:
   - one core course
   - one major course
   - six units of thesis

### Program-Specific Notes
- Admission to the MIH is through the DIH program.
- A student who completes the DIH unit requirements may proceed to the master’s program by taking additional 12 units, subject to admission requirements and the Qualifying Exam.
- To remain in the program, a student must:
  - pass all courses
  - not receive a grade of “5.0” in any course
- The UPOU retention policy applies to this program.
- The Master’s program consists of:
  - 6 units of thesis
  - 30 units of coursework total
  - 4 core courses (12 units)
  - 4 major courses (12 units)
  - 2 cognate courses (6 units)
- 24 units of the coursework are earned from the Diploma program.

### FAQs
Q: How do I get admitted to the Master of International Health program?
A: Admission is through the Diploma in International Health (DIH) program. After completing the 24-unit diploma requirements, you may proceed to the master’s program if you meet the MIH admission requirements and pass the Qualifying Exam.

Q: What must I do to stay in the program?
A: You must pass all courses and must not incur a grade of “5.0” in any course. The UPOU retention policy also applies.
