## Topic: Deferment of Admission - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
An applicant is admitted for a specific semester/term. If the applicant cannot enter the University in the term applied for, they may request deferment of admission to a later term.

### Admission Requirements
- The applicant must have already been granted admission for a specific semester/term.
- Deferment is a request made by the admitted applicant.

### Application Procedure
1. Submit a request for deferment of admission.
2. Ensure the request is made not later than one month after the start of the term to which the student was first admitted.
3. If the deferred admission is to be activated, write the University at least two months before the beginning of the term in which registration is desired.
4. Alternatively, write the University at least two months before the end of the 12-month deferment period to confirm registration in the coming term.
5. If the applicant fails to confirm registration within the required period, they must re-apply for admission.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Deferment request deadline: not later than one month after the start of the original term of admission.
- Maximum deferment period: 12 months from the original term of admission.
- Confirmation of deferred admission: at least two months before the beginning of the intended registration term, or at least two months before the end of the 12-month deferment period.
- “Start of the term” refers to the first day of classes as stated in the official university calendar.

### Program-Specific Notes
- No program-specific requirements mentioned.
- This policy applies to admission deferment generally.

### FAQs
Q: What happens if I do not confirm my deferred admission on time?
A: You will have to re-apply for admission.

Q: How long can admission be deferred?
A: Admission may be deferred for a maximum of 12 months from the original term of admission.
