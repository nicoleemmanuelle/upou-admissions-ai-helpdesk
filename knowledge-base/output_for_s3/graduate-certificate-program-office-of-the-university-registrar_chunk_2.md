## Topic: Graduate Certificate Program - Office of the University Registrar

### Overview
This page provides the admissions requirements and application submission instructions for the Graduate Certificate Program.

### Admission Requirements
- Signed hard copy of the application form generated from the Online Application System.
- Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with the school’s dry seal, imprint, and the Registrar’s original ink signature.
- If multiple schools or degrees were attended/earned, submit the OTR from the most recent school, and it must reflect all credentials from previous schools/degrees, including completed and not completed programs.
- Two (2) Letters of Recommendation using UPOU Form 1a.
  - Recommenders may be an employer/supervisor, former professor, or former program adviser.
- Original copy of birth certificate issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA).
- One (1) 2" x 2" ID photo.
- Scanned copy of the OTR uploaded in the Online Application System.
- 500-word essay stating:
  - reason for applying to UPOU’s distance education programs, and
  - plans after completing graduate studies.
- Proof of payment of the non-refundable application fee.
- NSO or PSA-issued birth certificate.
- Government-issued identification cards.

### Application Procedure
1. Complete the application form in the Online Application System.
2. Print and sign the generated application form.
3. Prepare the required hard-copy documents for mailing to the Office of the University Registrar.
4. Upload the required digital documents in the Online Application System.
5. Arrange for two recommendation letters to be submitted using UPOU Form 1a:
   - mailed to the Admission Section, Office of the University Registrar, UP Open University, Los Baños, Laguna 4031 Philippines; or
   - emailed by the recommender using their institutional email address to [email protected].
6. Pay the non-refundable application fee and upload the proof of payment.
7. Submit the application and required documents.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- Photo must be taken within the last 4 months.
- Application fee is non-refundable.

### Fees
- PHP 500.00 for Filipino citizens.
- USD 100.00 for non-Filipino citizens.

### Program-Specific Notes
- For foreign applicants:
  - Submit a copy of the passport’s data page showing date and place of birth.
  - Submit a birth certificate or equivalent authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post.
- Upon admission to the university, students must submit hard copies of:
  - Original Official Transcript of Records (OTR) with valid remarks.
- For applicants with international scholastic records:
  - Submit the original records authenticated by the Philippine Foreign Service Post in the applicant’s country of origin or legal residence.
- Photo requirements:
  - Size: 2×2 inches
  - Colored with a neutral white or off-white background
  - Taken within the last 4 months
  - Full face centered, eyes visible, with no eyeglasses or accessories obscuring features
  - Handwritten name tag in the format: First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, and Extension Name (if any), with signature over printed name
