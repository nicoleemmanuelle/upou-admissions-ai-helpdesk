## Topic: Master of Social Work - Faculty of Management and Development Studies

### Overview
- The provided content focuses on Field Instruction (FI), which is a required part of the Master of Social Work program.
- FI is conducted in collaboration with social agencies and is intended to integrate theoretical and experiential learning.
- The Social Work profession requires a minimum of 1,000 hours of practical training in an established social work agency under qualified supervision.

### Admission Requirements
- No general admission requirements were stated in the provided content.
- Program progression requirement: only students who have completed their core and specialization/survey courses may take FI subjects.
- Exception: for SW 280.1, students must complete only the core courses before enrolling.

### Application Procedure
- No application procedure for admission was stated in the provided content.
- For FI placement, the Program Chair acting as FI Coordinator:
  1. Develops the FI manual/guide and policies.
  2. Looks for and approves FI agencies.
  3. Oversees student supervision.
  4. Facilitates coordination between UPOU and the agency.
  5. Addresses FI-related concerns.

### Important Dates and Timelines
- No specific admission deadlines or enrollment dates were stated.
- FI requires a minimum of 1,000 hours of practical training, as referenced by the Social Work Law.

### Program-Specific Notes
- FI is an integral component of social work preparation.
- A school fieldwork supervisor is required to provide weekly supervision and serve as the faculty-in-charge (FIC) for the FI subject.
- An agency fieldwork supervisor from the placement agency supervises day-to-day activities.
- The agency fieldwork supervisor should have at least a master’s degree in any development discipline and a social work degree at any level.
- FI agencies are selected and approved by the FI Coordinator based on these minimum considerations:
  - The agency’s interest in providing field instruction to students.
  - Preferable affiliation with a licensed and accredited institution/agency or other accredited organizations/programs.
  - Existing programs and services that meet the expectations/objectives of the fieldwork.
  - At least one qualified and willing licensed social worker to provide instruction/supervision.

### FAQs
Q: What is the minimum practical training required in social work?
A: The Social Work Law requires a minimum of 1,000 hours of practical training in an established social work agency under qualified supervision.

Q: Who approves FI agencies?
A: The Program Chair, acting as FI Coordinator, selects and approves FI agencies.

Q: When can students take FI subjects?
A: Students may take FI subjects only after completing their core and specialization/survey courses, except for SW 280.1, which requires completion of core courses only.
